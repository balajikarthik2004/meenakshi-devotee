## A note on `ayyappan.webp`

Commons carries no suitably licensed image of Ayyappan, so that slot uses a photograph of
a temple mandapam rather than misattributing another deity's likeness. Replace it with the
temple's own sannidhi photograph before this goes anywhere near production.
