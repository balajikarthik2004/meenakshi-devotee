## Attribution obligations

`rajagopuram.webp` (CC BY 2.0), `annadanam.jpg` and `vedic-school.jpg` (both CC BY-SA
3.0) require attribution wherever they are displayed. The CC0 file does not, and is
credited here as a courtesy.

`vedic-school.jpg` shows identifiable children at a school with no connection to this
temple. That is a further reason — beyond the licence — to replace it with the
Devasthanam's own photography, taken with the parents' consent, before launch.

## Before production

These are stand-ins showing the *kind* of work each project covers — they are not
photographs of Sri Meenakshi Temple's own sites. Replace every one with the
Devasthanam's own photography of the actual work before this goes live; a project page
showing somebody else's building is the one thing on it a devotee could reasonably
call misleading. Setting `imageUrl` on the project in `mock/projects.ts` is the only
change needed.
